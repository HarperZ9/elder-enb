[center][size=5][b][color=#D4AF37]Installation[/color][/b][/size][/center]

[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]Step 1: ENB Binary Setup[/color][/b][/size]

[b][color=#D4AF37]Critical:[/color][/b] You must use the [b]Wrapper version[/b] of ENB, not the Injector version.

[list=1]
[*]Download the latest ENB Binary from [url=http://enbdev.com/]enbdev.com[/url]
[*]Open the downloaded archive and navigate to the [b]WrapperVersion[/b] folder
[*]Extract [b][color=#6DB3F2]ONLY d3d11.dll[/color][/b] to your Skyrim game directory (where SkyrimSE.exe is located)
[*]Do [b]NOT[/b] extract any other files from the ENB archive - these are provided by Elder ENB
[/list]

[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]Step 2: Before You Begin[/color][/b][/size]

Delete all remaining files from previous ENB configurations and clear your ENB shader cache (typically located in your [b]Overwrite[/b] folder or Skyrim directory). Install all requirements and their prerequisites.

[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]Step 3: Standard Installation[/color][/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Download and extract [b]Elder ENB - Universal (FINAL).zip[/b]
[*]Copy [b]enbseries.ini[/b] and [b]enblocal.ini[/b] to your game directory
[*]Copy the contents of the [b]enbseries[/b] folder into your pre-existing enbseries folder
[*]Install [b]Required Files (FINAL).zip[/b] using Mod Organizer 2
[*]Launch the game and press [b]Shift + Enter[/b] to verify ENB is enabled
[*]Press [b]END[/b] to confirm your KreatE menu opens correctly
[/list]

[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]Wabbajack Installation[/color][/b][/size]

For Wabbajack lists using Root Builder:
[list=1]
[*]Install both zip files through Mod Organizer 2
[*]Create a [b]Root[/b] folder inside the mod directory
[*]Move enbseries folder, enbseries.ini, and enblocal.ini into Root
[*]Move Silent Horizons - Shader Core contents into Root as well
[*]Clear your ENB Shader Cache
[/list]

[color=#D4AF37][b]Note:[/b][/color] Support is provided for Mod Organizer 2 installations only.

[center][size=5][b][color=#D4AF37]Preset Overlay System[/color][/b][/size]

[i]151 configurations to create your perfect visual experience[/i][/center]

Preset Overlays modify shader parameters on top of the base ENB configuration. Think of them as "layers" that adjust various aspects of the visual experience. Within the ENB menu, the right-hand pane contains all overlay presets.

[b][color=#D4AF37]Usage Rules:[/color][/b]
[list]
[*]Enable [b]ONE[/b] overlay per category (QUALITY, STYLE, DOF, BLOOM, etc.)
[*][b]PREFERENCES, LIGHTING, and LOOK[/b] categories may have up to [b]2[/b] enabled
[*]Use the [b]Amount slider[/b] to blend effects from 0-100%
[/list]

[center][size=4][b][color=#D4AF37]ENV (144-151)[/color][/b][/size]
[i]Environment Lighting Ratios[/i][/center]

Comprehensive adjustments combining direct/ambient ratios, sky gradients, skylighting, and image-based lighting:

[list]
[*][b]Photorealistic[/b] - Balanced natural daylight (~5:1) for authentic scenes
[*][b]Soft Diffused[/b] - Portrait-friendly (~2:1) with open shadows
[*][b]Harsh Dramatic[/b] - High contrast (~8:1) with deep shadows
[*][b]Overcast Flat[/b] - Even illumination (~1.2:1) simulating clouds
[*][b]Golden Hour Enhanced[/b] - Boosted sunrise/sunset (~6:1)
[*][b]Moody Atmospheric[/b] - Darker overall (~4:1) mysterious mood
[*][b]Bright Airy[/b] - Lifted exposure (~2.5:1) optimistic atmosphere
[*][b]Cinematic Contrast[/b] - Film-like balance (~4.3:1) professional lighting
[/list]

[center][size=5][b][color=#D4AF37]Creating Your Perfect Look[/color][/b][/size][/center]

The power of this system lies in combination:

[list=1]
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]STYLE[/color][/b] - Choose your base color philosophy
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]LOOK[/color][/b] - Layer a film emulation for character
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]ENV[/color][/b] - Set your lighting character
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]LIGHTING[/color][/b] - Match your lighting mod setup
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]DOF[/color][/b] - Pick a depth of field style
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]BLOOM[/color][/b] - Adjust light behavior
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]GRAIN + LENS + VIGNETTE[/color][/b] - Add film-like character
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]CINEMATIC BORDERS[/color][/b] - Optional theatrical framing
[/list]

Use the [b]Amount sliders[/b] to blend each overlay - you don't have to go 100%. Sometimes 30% of a LOOK combined with 50% of a STYLE creates something unique.
