[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]Overview[/color][/b][/size]

Elder ENB includes a [b]Preset Overlay System[/b] with [b][color=#D4AF37]163 carefully crafted configurations[/color][/b] that can be mixed and matched to create your perfect visual experience. Think of overlays as "layers" that adjust various aspects of the visual experience without requiring manual tweaking.

Within the ENB menu, the right-hand pane displays all overlay presets. Some introduce minor changes, while others transform the entire look. Combined with KreatE presets and LOOK LUTs, the possibilities are endless.

[b][color=#D4AF37]Usage Rules:[/color][/b]
[list]
[*]Enable [b]ONE overlay per category[/b] (ENV, QUALITY, STYLE, DOF, BLOOM, CINEMATIC, SSAO, GRAIN, LENS, VIGNETTE, BLUR)
[*][b]PREFERENCES, LIGHTING, and LOOK[/b] may have up to [b]2 enabled simultaneously[/b]
[*]Use the [b]Amount slider[/b] to blend effects from 0% to 100%
[/list]

[size=5][b][color=#D4AF37]Creating Your Perfect Look[/color][/b][/size]

The power of this system lies in combination:

[list=1]
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Start with ENV or STYLE[/color][/b] - Choose your base (e.g., "Nordic Light" or "Arrival of Autumn")
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Add a LOOK[/color][/b] - Layer film emulation (e.g., "Kodak Kodachrome 64")
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Adjust LIGHTING[/color][/b] - Match your lighting mod setup
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Fine-tune with DOF[/color][/b] - Pick your depth of field style
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Consider BLOOM[/color][/b] - Adjust light behavior to taste
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Add GRAIN, LENS, VIGNETTE[/color][/b] - For film-like character
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Optional: CINEMATIC BORDERS[/color][/b] - For theatrical presentation
[/list]

Use the [b]Amount sliders[/b] to blend each overlay's contribution. Sometimes 30% of a LOOK combined with 50% of a STYLE creates something unique that neither could achieve alone.
