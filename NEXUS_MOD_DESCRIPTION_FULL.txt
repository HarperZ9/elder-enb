----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]Before You Begin[/color][/b][/size]

[b][color=#D4AF37]Important:[/color][/b] Prior to installation, always delete all remaining files from previous ENB configurations and clear your ENB shader cache. The cache is typically located in your [b]Overwrite[/b] folder or your Skyrim directory.

Install all requirements and their prerequisites, carefully following the installation instructions provided by each mod author.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]ENB Binary Installation[/color][/b][/size]

[b][color=#D4AF37]Critical:[/color][/b] You must use the [b][color=#6DB3F2]Wrapper version[/color][/b] of ENB, not the Injector version.

[list=1]
[*]Download the latest ENB Binary from [url=http://enbdev.com/]enbdev.com[/url]
[*]Open the downloaded archive and navigate to the [b]WrapperVersion[/b] folder
[*]Extract [b][color=#6DB3F2]ONLY d3d11.dll[/color][/b] to your Skyrim game directory (where SkyrimSE.exe is located)
[*]Do [b]NOT[/b] extract any other files from the ENB archive (enblocal.ini, enbseries folder, etc.) - these are provided by Elder ENB
[/list]

[b][color=#D4AF37]Note:[/color][/b] Installing any files other than d3d11.dll from the ENB Binary will overwrite Elder ENB's configuration. Only the d3d11.dll plugin is needed.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]Standard Installation[/color][/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Download and extract [b][color=#6DB3F2]Elder ENB - Universal (FINAL).zip[/color][/b] from Nexus Mods
[*]Copy [b]enbseries.ini[/b] and [b]enblocal.ini[/b] to your game directory
[*]Copy the contents of the [b]enbseries[/b] folder into your pre-existing enbseries folder (provided by Silent Horizons 2 - Shader Core)
[*]Install [b][color=#6DB3F2]Required Files (FINAL).zip[/color][/b] using Mod Organizer 2 or Vortex
[*]Launch the game and press [b]Shift + Enter[/b] to verify ENB is enabled
[*]Press [b]END[/b] to confirm your KreatE menu opens correctly
[/list]

[color=#D4AF37][b]Note:[/b][/color] Support is provided for Mod Organizer 2 installations only. Vortex and manual installations are at your own discretion.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]Wabbajack Installation[/color][/b][/size]

Wabbajack lists typically utilize [b]Root Builder[/b], which changes how mods are loaded to and from the game directory.

[list=1]
[*]Install both [b]Elder ENB - Universal (FINAL).zip[/b] and [b]Required Files (FINAL).zip[/b] through Mod Organizer 2
[*]Open the mod folder in File Explorer
[*]Create a new folder named [b][color=#6DB3F2]Root[/color][/b] inside the mod directory
[*]Move [b]only[/b] the enbseries folder, enbseries.ini, and enblocal.ini into this newly created Root folder
[*]Move the contents of [b]Silent Horizons - Shader Core[/b] into this Root folder as well
[*]Clear your ENB Shader Cache (typically located in your Overwrite directory)
[/list]

[size=5][center][b][color=#D4AF37]Preset Overlays - Your Personal Customization System[/color][/b][/center][/size]

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Elder ENB - Universal includes a [b][color=#6DB3F2]Preset Overlay System[/color][/b] with [b][color=#D4AF37]151 carefully crafted configurations[/color][/b] that can be mixed and matched to create your perfect visual experience. This system represents my personal journey into understanding image composition, color grading, and shader development—a labor of love designed to give you the freedom to make Skyrim truly your own.

[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]How Preset Overlays Work[/color][/b][/size]

Preset Overlays modify specific shader parameters on top of the base ENB configuration. Think of them as "layers" that adjust various aspects of the visual experience without requiring you to manually tweak individual values.

Within the ENB menu, on the right-hand side is a pane that includes all overlay presets. Some introduce minor changes, while others are like installing an entirely different ENB altogether. Using these in tandem with KreatE presets and LOOK presets introduces a wide range of potential variation in game appearance and style.

[b][color=#D4AF37]Important Usage Rules:[/color][/b]
[list]
[*]Enable [b][color=#6DB3F2]ONE overlay per category prefix[/color][/b] (QUALITY, STYLE, DOF, BLOOM, CINEMATIC BORDERS, SSAO, GRAIN, LENS, VIGNETTE, BLUR, ENV)
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]PREFERENCES, LIGHTING, and LOOK[/color][/b] categories may have [b][color=#D4AF37]up to 2 enabled simultaneously[/color][/b]
[*]Use the [b]Amount slider[/b] in the ENB GUI to blend overlay effects from 0% to 100%
[/list]

[size=4][b][color=#D4AF37]STYLE Overlays (10-44) - KreatE Color Philosophies[/color][/b][/size]

These overlays embody distinct visual identities, each inspired by different artistic approaches to color grading and atmosphere. Select one to transform your entire Skyrim experience.

[size=3][b][color=#6DB3F2]Classic Styles (10-17)[/color][/b][/size]

[b]10.28.16 (SE)[/b]
[i]Clean, neutral look inspired by vanilla Skyrim SE. Balanced exposure with subtle enhancements.[/i]
The date marks Skyrim Special Edition's release—this style captures that refined, neutral foundation. Slightly reduced exposure compensation keeps things grounded with subtle contrast adjustments. Perfect for those who want ENB quality without dramatic color shifts.

[b]11.11.11 (LE)[/b]
[i]Classic warm tones inspired by Legendary Edition. Nostalgic feel with golden warmth.[/i]
Named for the original Skyrim release date, this style brings back the golden, amber-tinged memories of 2011. Warm color temperature shifts, enhanced saturation in earth tones, and a gentle lift to the shadows create that nostalgic atmosphere many veteran players remember.

[b]Autumn's Arrival[/b]
[i]Golden, nostalgic tones with warm amber highlights. Emphasizes fall colors and atmospheric warmth.[/i]
Inspired by the changing seasons, this style pushes warm orange and amber into the highlights while maintaining cool shadow detail. Increased color temperature and enhanced saturation in the red-yellow spectrum make autumn forests and sunsets stunning.

[b]Ethqnm Edition[/b]
[i]Vibrant, cinematic look with punchy colors and strong contrast. Dynamic and eye-catching.[/i]
Bold, unapologetic color. This style cranks up saturation and contrast for a vivid, larger-than-life aesthetic. Aggressive highlight treatment creates images that pop off the screen—perfect for those who want Skyrim to feel like a high-budget fantasy film.

[b]Jotunheimar[/b]
[i]Cold, icy atmosphere with blue-shifted tones. Harsh Nordic winter aesthetic.[/i]
Named for the land of frost giants, this style desaturates warm tones while pushing blues and cyans. Reduced exposure compensation creates darker, moodier scenes, while the color temperature shift makes even sunny days feel cold. Ideal for Nordic-focused playthroughs.

[b]Rudy Inspired[/b]
[i]Natural, documentary style with realistic colors. Clean and authentic looking.[/i]
Inspired by the naturalistic approach of documentary cinematography. Minimal color grading intervention, balanced contrast, and true-to-life saturation. This style prioritizes authenticity over stylization—what you might call "photographically honest."

[b]Somber Tones[/b]
[i]Muted, ethereal atmosphere with soft contrast. Dreamy and melancholic aesthetic.[/i]
Reduced saturation and softened contrast create a painterly, melancholic quality. Lifted blacks and compressed highlights give everything an ethereal, dream-like quality perfect for contemplative playthroughs.

[b]Wretched[/b]
[i]Bleak, desaturated horror atmosphere. Dark and oppressive with crushed blacks.[/i]
For those seeking darkness. Heavy desaturation, crushed shadows, and oppressive vignetting transform Skyrim into something haunting. Perfect for survival horror playthroughs.

[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Moody Atmospheric[/color][/b] - Darker overall (~4:1 ratio) with mysterious, cinematic mood
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Bright Airy[/color][/b] - Lifted exposure (~2.5:1 ratio) for light, optimistic atmosphere
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Cinematic Contrast[/color][/b] - Film-like balance (~4.3:1 ratio) with professional cinema lighting
[/list]

[size=4][b][color=#D4AF37]Creating Your Perfect Look[/color][/b][/size]

The power of this system lies in combination. Here's an example workflow:

[list=1]
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Start with a STYLE[/color][/b] - Choose your base color philosophy (e.g., "Autumn's Arrival")
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Add a LOOK[/color][/b] - Layer a film emulation for character (e.g., "Kodak Kodachrome 64")
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Set your ENV[/color][/b] - Choose your lighting character (e.g., "Photorealistic" or "Cinematic Contrast")
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Adjust LIGHTING[/color][/b] if needed - Match your lighting mod setup
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Fine-tune with DOF[/color][/b] - Pick a depth of field style that suits your preference
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Consider BLOOM[/color][/b] - Adjust light behavior to taste
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Add GRAIN, LENS, VIGNETTE[/color][/b] - For film-like character
[*][b][color=#6DB3F2]Optional: CINEMATIC BORDERS[/color][/b] - For that extra theatrical touch
[/list]

Use the [b]Amount sliders[/b] to blend each overlay's contribution—you don't have to go 100% on everything. Sometimes 30% of a LOOK combined with 50% of a STYLE creates something unique that neither could achieve alone.
