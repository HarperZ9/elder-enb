[center][size=5][b][color=#D4AF37]Design Philosophy[/color][/b][/size][/center]

Elder ENB is built on a foundational principle: [b]separation of concerns[/b]. Rather than offering a single locked-in visual style, the system divides visual control into distinct layers that work together harmoniously:

[list]
[*][b]KreatE Weather Presets[/b] - The atmospheric foundation (sky, sun, clouds, fog, ambient light)
[*][b]Preset Overlays[/b] - Stackable adjustments for style, lighting, effects, and post-processing
[/list]

This architecture means you're never locked into a single look. Change your entire atmosphere by switching KreatE presets, or fine-tune specific aspects through overlays - all without compromising the carefully calibrated baseline.

[center][size=5][b][color=#D4AF37]KreatE Weather Presets[/color][/b][/size]

[i]The Atmospheric Foundation - 38 Unique Visual Atmospheres[/i][/center]

[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]Core Design Principles[/color][/b][/size]

KreatE presets don't simply adjust colors - they rebuild the entire atmospheric system from the ground up. Each preset contains 724 meticulously crafted configuration files covering every aspect of Skyrim's environmental rendering.

[b]Color Coherence[/b]
Every color in a KreatE preset flows from a unified palette. Sun color influences sunlight, which influences cloud illumination, which influences sky gradients. This isn't arbitrary - it follows how light actually behaves:

[list]
[*][b]DALC (Directional Ambient Light Colors)[/b] - Top derives from SkyUpper, Middle blends SkyLower with Horizon, Bottom reflects FogNear
[*][b]Cloud Colors[/b] - Automatically aligned with Sun and Sunlight values for natural illumination
[*][b]Sky Gradients[/b] - Upper, Lower, and Horizon colors maintain proper atmospheric perspective
[/list]

[b]Time-of-Day Consistency[/b]
Each preset defines colors for six time periods: Dawn, Sunrise, Day, Sunset, Dusk, and Night. Transitions between these periods are smooth and natural, with careful attention to how colors shift as the sun moves.

[b]Weather-Specific Tuning[/b]
Clear skies behave differently than overcast conditions. Fog weathers receive 33% ambient reduction for proper visibility. Snow and rain conditions adjust bloom and particle effects appropriately.

[center][size=4][b][color=#D4AF37]ENV - Environment Lighting[/color][/b][/size]
[i]The Technical Foundation of Light[/i][/center]

ENV overlays control the fundamental physics of light in your game world. These aren't color filters - they modify how light interacts with surfaces, how shadows form, and how the atmosphere scatters illumination.

[b]Core Parameters:[/b]
[list]
[*][b]ColorPow[/b] - Controls color intensity response (baseline 1.4). Lower values produce richer saturation; higher values increase contrast punch.
[*][b]DirectLightingIntensity[/b] - Sunlight/moonlight strength across all six time periods
[*][b]AmbientLightingIntensity[/b] - Fill light from the sky dome
[*][b]DirectLightingCurve[/b] - Falloff behavior of direct illumination
[*][b]FogCurveMultiplier[/b] - Atmospheric depth and haze
[/list]

[b]Available ENV Overlays:[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Golden Hour[/b] - Reduced ColorPow (-0.25 to -0.30) for rich, saturated golden tones. Enhanced volumetric fog during transitions. Warm color filters throughout.

[*][b]Deep Contrast[/b] - Elevated ColorPow (+0.15 to +0.20) for punchy, dramatic lighting. Increased direct lighting curves, reduced ambient. Professional moody aesthetic.

[*][b]Nordic Light[/b] - Subtle cool balance with slight ColorPow increase. Captures soft Scandinavian diffusion with maintained shadow detail.

[*][b]Cinematic Drama[/b] - Strong contrast ratios with controlled highlights. Film-like lighting balance optimized for storytelling moments.

[*][b]Fantasy Vivid[/b] - Lower ColorPow for lush color rendering. Enhanced bloom during transitions. Magical atmosphere without oversaturation.

[*][b]Natural Documentary[/b] - Neutral baseline. No ColorPow modifications. Authenticity-focused for realistic gameplay.

[*][b]Lifted Shadows[/b] - Reduced ColorPow with elevated ambient. Better shadow visibility without sacrificing depth.

[*][b]Linear Light[/b] - Linear response curve modifications for HDR-like rendering. Technical accuracy over stylization.
[/list]

[center][size=4][b][color=#D4AF37]STYLE - KreatE Color Philosophies[/color][/b][/size]
[i]Complete Visual Identities[/i][/center]

STYLE overlays are comprehensive visual packages that transform the entire color pipeline. Each represents a complete aesthetic philosophy - from tonemapping through color grading to final output.

Unlike ENV overlays which focus on lighting physics, STYLE overlays work in the color domain: saturation, contrast, tint, vibrance, and tonal relationships.

[b]What STYLE Overlays Control:[/b]

[center][size=5][b][color=#D4AF37]Building Your Look[/color][/b][/size][/center]

The power of this system lies in intentional layering:

[list=1]
[*][b]Start with KreatE[/b] - Choose your atmospheric foundation
[*][b]Add ENV[/b] - Set your lighting physics (ColorPow, intensities, curves)
[*][b]Apply STYLE[/b] - Define your color philosophy
[*][b]Layer LOOK[/b] - Optional film stock character
[*][b]Set LIGHTING[/b] - Match your lighting mod configuration
[*][b]Choose DOF + BLOOM[/b] - Define focus and light behavior
[*][b]Add finishing touches[/b] - Grain, lens, vignette as desired
[/list]

[b]Example Combinations:[/b]

[code]
The Survivalist
├── KreatE: The Revenant
├── ENV: Deep Contrast
├── STYLE: Nordic Saga
├── LIGHTING: Darker Nights
└── LOOK: None (preserve desaturation)

Fantasy Adventure
├── KreatE: Vibrant Fantasy
├── ENV: Fantasy Vivid
├── STYLE: Vibrant Fantasy
├── BLOOM: Ethereal
└── LOOK: Fuji Velvia 50

Cinematic Horror
├── KreatE: Wretched
├── ENV: Cinematic Drama
├── STYLE: Gothic Shadows
├── LIGHTING: Darker Interiors
└── VIGNETTE: Heavy Dramatic
[/code]

Use the [b]Amount sliders[/b] - 30% of a LOOK combined with 60% of a STYLE often creates something more nuanced than either at full strength.

[center][size=5][b][color=#D4AF37]Installation[/color][/b][/size][/center]

[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]Step 1: ENB Binary[/color][/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Download the latest ENB Binary from [url=http://enbdev.com/]enbdev.com[/url]
[*]Open the archive, navigate to [b]WrapperVersion[/b]
[*]Extract [b]ONLY d3d11.dll[/b] to your Skyrim game directory
[*]Do NOT extract any other files - Elder ENB provides the rest
[/list]

[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]Step 2: Installation[/color][/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Delete all files from previous ENB configurations
[*]Clear your ENB shader cache (Overwrite folder)
[*]Install all requirements and prerequisites
[*]Copy enbseries.ini and enblocal.ini to your game directory
[*]Copy enbseries folder contents to your existing enbseries folder
[*]Install Required Files via Mod Organizer 2
[/list]

[size=4][b][color=#6DB3F2]Step 3: Verification[/color][/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Launch the game
[*]Press [b]Shift + Enter[/b] to verify ENB is active
[*]Press [b]END[/b] to confirm KreatE menu opens
[*]Select your desired KreatE preset
[*]Configure overlays in the ENB menu's right-hand pane
[/list]
